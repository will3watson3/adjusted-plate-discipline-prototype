# Contact XGBoost v1: frozen comparison contract

Specified November 16, 2026 before candidate training/evaluation.

Goal: compare XGBoost to the existing contact GAM with exactly the same predictor information, labels and chronological histories. This concerns P(contact | ordinary swing); no BIP model or Decision+ scoring change is included. The active scorer/reference remain fixed.

Primary benchmark: saved updated-prior-season population contact GAM fitted on 2022–23. Secondary benchmark: active contact GAM with frozen 2021 histories, whose feature values therefore differ. No player IDs, source zones, outcome variables, additional histories, or extra predictors enter XGBoost. Use exactly the unique RHS variables in the updated GAM formula, including its missingness indicators. Factors receive full one-hot encoding; numeric values and imputations match the GAM. Tree interactions replace GAM smooth terms; the functional forms differ but the available predictor information is identical.

All models evaluate exactly the same eligible ordinary swings with valid core inputs. Labels: hit_into_play/foul/foul_tip = contact; swinging_strike/swinging_strike_blocked = whiff. Histories use completed season y−1 (2022→2021, 2023→2022, 2024→2023, 2025→2024), retaining the existing 100/50-swing profile pooling. Unknown player identities use existing profile fallback; identities themselves are excluded as features.

Development: train on 2022, validate on 2023. Fit medians, levels and category references only on 2022. Missing-indicator schema is fixed from the benchmark formula; no future medians or levels are used. No random pitch split. Three prespecified candidates differ only in max_depth = 3, 4, 5. Fixed settings: binary:logistic, logloss, CPU histogram, eta=.05, min_child_weight=50, lambda=10, alpha=1, subsample=.8, colsample_bytree=.9, max_bin=256, two CPU threads, seed=20261116, no weighting or class balancing. Early stopping after 60 rounds without validation improvement, maximum 1200 rounds. Choose minimum 2023 validation log loss; exact ties favor shallower depth. Record all training/validation curves and whether the budget cap binds. No post-test search or recalibration.

Refit the chosen depth on all 2022–23 swings for exactly the chosen number of rounds, using the saved GAM's final training preprocessing. Freeze model/config/feature schema and hashes before opening 2024/2025 evaluation predictions. 2024 is secondary descriptive replication only and cannot change the selected candidate. 2025 is the requested primary later-season comparison; it has been inspected in other project experiments and is explicitly exploratory, not a pristine pipeline holdout.

Primary outcome: 2025 log loss against updated-history GAM. Also report Brier, calibration intercept/slope/bins, mean bias and AUC, identical-cohort paired game bootstrap (500 resamples; conditional on fits), and descriptive slices by count, pitch family, handedness, prior-profile availability and training hitter exposure. A factual improvement is supported if the paired primary loss interval is below zero; calibration/Brier/slices can qualify interpretation. No automatic adoption. Better factual contact prediction does not establish improved counterfactual swing valuation or causal decision talent.

Verification: exact feature allowlist, training-only development preprocessing, history chronology, unique identical cohorts, probability validity, label/outcome independence, row/batch invariance, serialization and deterministic training on a small fixture, saved-versus-rebuilt predictions, metric/bin/slice/bootstrap reconstruction, original artifact hashes. Confidence comes from this evidence; absence of overfitting cannot be guaranteed.

Method references: https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/parameter.html and https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/r_docs/R-package/docs/reference/xgb.train.html . Installed R xgboost API/version is recorded in sessionInfo; local help establishes one-based R best_iteration and save_best behavior.
