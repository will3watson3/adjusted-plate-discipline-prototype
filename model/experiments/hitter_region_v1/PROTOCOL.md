# Hitter-region v1: fixed protocol

Created 2026-11-02 before inspecting this experiment's 2024 results. Fit every stage on 2022–2023. Evaluate on previously inspected, exploratory 2024; no outcome-based model selection, recalibration, or tuning. This is an isolated experiment, not an activation of Decision+.

A is the existing selected GAM formula/family/target with updated prior-season histories. B uses exactly A's fitted linear predictor plus one Gaussian hitter-by-source-zone random intercept. No additional overall hitter or pitcher effects, region main effects, or pitch-type interactions are included. Coefficients of the GAM are fixed for B. B is therefore a residual-offset approximation rather than the preferred joint GAM fit.

The machine has 16 GiB of physical memory. About 9,000 hitter-region groups occur per season; the full contact design would exceed memory. mgcv's ordinary random-effect smooth representation does not exploit the sparse random-effect structure (see [mgcv documentation](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/mgcv/html/random.effects.html)). The report records the actual full-design memory estimate for each stage. Instead, the implementation accumulates group gradients and Hessians without allocating that design. Binomial-logit stages use Laplace marginal likelihood; BIP value retains the quasi-Poisson log link and uses the corresponding scaled quasi-likelihood with dispersion fixed at A's estimate. This quasi-marginal objective is an approximation, not a probability density for fractional run values. A single population variance is estimated independently for each stage on training data. There is no added global intercept.

For each variance, Newton iterations solve the penalized group modes. The scalar objective is negative summed (quasi-)log likelihood plus sum(u²)/(2 tau²) plus 0.5 sum(log(1 + tau² times likelihood curvature)). Compare the optimized objective with the exact zero-variance boundary. Numerical search limits are tau from 0.0001 to 4; an upper-bound solution is an error, not a selected cap. Coefficients are conditional modes. Unseen hitter-region combinations, missing hitter identities, and missing/invalid regions receive exactly zero adjustment, hence the control prediction. Invalid regions are retained for the control and evaluation but excluded from learning regional effects.

The custom sparse solver is checked independently against lme4's binomial Laplace fit and a dense mgcv quasi-Poisson random-effect smooth on synthetic data. Tests also cover sparse shrinkage, fallback, and chronological profile equivalence. Two-stage estimation ignores uncertainty in the learned GAM offsets and can shrink regional effects too strongly when the GAM already absorbs their training signal. The fixed quasi dispersion adds another limitation. This implementation is appropriate for a simple exploratory baseline, not final inference on player talent.

A full joint random-effect bam fit was not attempted. Its discrete implementation can avoid the full observation-by-coefficient design, so the reported full-design size is not a claim about bam peak memory. Dense coefficient algebra for roughly 10,000 random effects still presents a substantial computational burden; the offset approach is an explicit resource tradeoff, not evidence that a joint fit cannot work.

Histories are identical in both arms: 2022 rows use 2021, 2023 use 2022, and 2024 use 2023. Contact histories reuse cp_profiles/cp_features (100 swings toward league, 50 split swings toward player). BIP power profiles reuse pc_power_profiles (50 tracked BIP toward league, 30 split tracked BIP toward player), changing only the input season. Legacy internal field names batter_ev_2021 and pitcher_ev_2021 remain to preserve formulas; their contents are now prior-season EV. No current-pitch contact outcome, exit velocity, launch angle, or expected hitting statistic enters features. Training-data end dates and profile-source years are recorded per stage.

Regions use the source zone field categorically. The [official CSV documentation](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs) specifies the catcher's perspective and links the [official numbered diagram](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/sections/statcast-search-new/images/zones.png), inspected directly. The in-zone grid is 1/2/3 across the top, 4/5/6 across the middle, 7/8/9 across the bottom. Outer quadrants are 11 upper left, 12 upper right, 13 lower left, 14 lower right. There is no valid zone 10. The observed coordinate summaries independently check this orientation: negative plate_x is diagram-left and positive is diagram-right. These are source Gameday zones, not reconstructed attack zones. No thresholds are invented and no handedness reflection is applied. Existing continuous plate_x and normalized vertical smooths remain unchanged.

Factual comparison populations are ordinary swings, physical contacts, non-BIP contacts, and verified BIP immediate post-play targets respectively. The existing target quarantine and frozen RE288 tables are reused. Each arm has exactly the same keyed rows. Report log loss, Brier, calibration intercept/slope and bins for binary stages, and RMSE/MAE/mean error for BIP value. Slice by region, prior history availability and training stage-specific hitter-region count (0, 1–9, 10–49, 50–199, 200+); these are descriptive slices, not tuning parameters. Bootstrap whole games 500 times with a fixed seed, reporting candidate minus control. Intervals are conditional on fitted models; no training refits or multiple-comparison correction.

For descriptive Decision+ replay, reuse the saved active-v6 take values, policy probabilities, original support mask and original 2024 reference center/scale by pitch key. Apply the same existing range and target-range safeguard to the updated features/values, intersecting across arms; report any additional exclusions. Do not promote previously unsupported rows. Qualification remains >=500 supported pitches, >=60 supported games, >=70% coverage. The score remains (observed swing − frozen policy probability) times (Q_swing − Q_take), averaged per 100 supported pitches. Four-branch arithmetic and deterministic whiff/foul/caught-tip state values are unchanged. Raw unsupported extrapolations are retained in keyed audit files and excluded from primary score comparisons.

Do not carry forward old model intervals or preference labels. New hitter-change intervals resample games and cover sampling variation only, conditional on both fits. They do not include changed model/offset/RE/policy/reference uncertainty. Better factual prediction is not proof of better counterfactual decision evaluation. All original input hashes, active manifest, policy and reference artifacts must remain unchanged.
